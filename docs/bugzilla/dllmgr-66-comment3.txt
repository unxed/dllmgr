Result of the run on current wine.git master (wine-11.21, 2af4506cc3),
X11, 192 DPI prefix:

A probe renders every Latin and Cyrillic letter of Tahoma regular, bold and
simulated italic at 10, 11 and 13 px with GetGlyphOutline(GGO_BITMAP) and
records the inked columns and the advance of each glyph. On master the
result is identical to wine-11.0 for all 161 glyphs at every one of these
sizes (one italic glyph at 13 px differs). Compared with Windows (same
probe on Windows Server 2025):

  size  style    advances different from Windows (Latin / Cyrillic)
  10    regular   0 / 8
  10    bold     40 / 33
  10    italic   44 / 38
  11    regular   0 / 10
  11    bold     24 / 35
  11    italic   55 / 49

These are the sizes Photoshop CS2 uses for the places in comment 1
("Open" and the other history states in Tahoma Bold 10 px, "Background"
in simulated italic, the palette labels), and Wine draws them from the
embedded bitmap strikes, which aac722e8 does not change. So the report is
still valid on master.
